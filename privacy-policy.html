Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how FreeStream handles your information.

Last Updated: December 28, 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to FreeStream. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our streaming service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe online experience.

Important: FreeStream does not require registration or collect personal information. Your privacy is our priority.

2. Information We Don't Collect

FreeStream is designed to be privacy-focused. We do not collect or store:

Personal Information

We don't ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personal details.

Account Data

No registration required. No usernames, passwords, or account information stored.

Payment Information

FreeStream is completely free. No payment details, credit cards, or billing information.

Identity Data

No age, gender, location, or other identity markers are collected.

3. Information We Automatically Receive

Technical Information Only

Like all websites, our servers automatically receive certain technical information when you visit:

3.1 Browser Information

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)
  • Operating system
  • Screen resolution

3.2 Technical Data

  • IP address (anonymized for analytics)
  • Connection type and speed
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages visited and time spent

3.3 Usage Statistics

We collect aggregated, anonymous usage data to improve our service:

  • Most popular movies and shows
  • Peak usage times
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Error reports and performance data

4. Cookies and Tracking

4.1 Essential Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality:

  • Session cookies for basic site operations
  • Preference cookies for theme settings
  • Security cookies to prevent abuse

4.2 Third-Party Services

Some third-party services may use their own cookies:

  • Video players and streaming services
  • Analytics services (anonymous data only)
  • Content delivery networks

4.3 Cookie Control

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

FreeStream may use third-party services to enhance functionality:

5.1 Analytics Providers

We use anonymous analytics to understand usage patterns and improve our service. No personal data is shared.

5.2 Content Delivery

Video content is delivered through third-party streaming services. Their privacy policies apply to their services.

5.3 External Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Data Security

Security Measures

We implement security measures to protect the minimal data we handle:

6.1 Technical Security

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Regular security updates
  • Firewall protection
  • DDoS mitigation

6.2 Data Minimization

We follow the principle of data minimization - we only collect what is absolutely necessary for service operation.

6.3 No Data Storage

Since we don't collect personal information, there's no personal data to store or secure.

7. Children's Privacy

FreeStream is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7.1 Age Restrictions

Some content may be restricted to users 18 years or older. We rely on user honesty for age verification.

7.2 Parental Control

Parents and guardians are responsible for monitoring their children's internet usage and implementing parental controls.

8. Your Rights

8.1 Right to Information

You have the right to know what information we collect. This policy provides that information.

8.2 Right to Control

You can control cookie settings through your browser. You can also use privacy-focused browsers and extensions.

8.3 Right to Object

You can choose not to use our service if you disagree with our privacy practices.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

9.1 Notification

Continued use of FreeStream after policy updates constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

9.2 Review Responsibility

It is your responsibility to review this policy periodically for changes.
