DMCA Policy

Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Notice and Takedown Policy for FreeStream.

Last Updated: December 28, 2023

1. DMCA Compliance

FreeStream respects the intellectual property rights of others and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). This policy outlines our procedures for addressing claims of copyright infringement.

Important Notice

FreeStream does not host any content on its servers. We act as an index and directory for content available elsewhere on the internet. All content is provided by third-party sources.

2. Notice of Infringement

If you believe your copyrighted work has been infringed through our service, you may submit a written DMCA takedown notice containing the following information:

Required Information for DMCA Notice

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed
  2. URL of the allegedly infringing content on FreeStream
  3. Contact information including address, telephone number, and email
  4. Statement that you have a good faith belief the use is unauthorized
  5. Statement that the information in the notification is accurate
  6. Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent

2.1 Where to Send DMCA Notices

DMCA takedown notices should be sent to our designated agent:

  • Email: dmca@freestream.com
  • Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice
  • Response Time: We aim to respond within 48 hours

Note: Only complete DMCA notices containing all required information will be processed.

3. Counter-Notice Procedure

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice containing:

Required Information for Counter-Notice

  1. Identification of the material that was removed
  2. Statement under penalty of perjury of good faith belief of removal error
  3. Contact information including address, telephone number, and email
  4. Consent to jurisdiction in federal district court
  5. Consent to accept service of process from the notifier
  6. Physical or electronic signature

3.1 Counter-Notice Process

Upon receiving a valid counter-notice, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
  • Forward the counter-notice to the original complainant
  • Restore the content within 10-14 business days unless legal action is taken

4. Content Removal Process

4.1 Review Procedure

When we receive a valid DMCA notice, we will:

  • Review the notice for completeness and validity
  • Remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing content
  • Notify the content provider of the removal
  • Keep records of all DMCA notices and actions taken

4.2 Timelines

  • Initial Response: Within 48 hours of receiving notice
  • Content Removal: Within 24 hours of validating notice
  • Provider Notification: Within 48 hours of removal

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

Repeat Infringer Policy

FreeStream maintains a policy of terminating accounts of repeat infringers. Users who repeatedly infringe copyrights will have their access to our service terminated.

5.1 Infringer Tracking

We maintain records of:

  • All DMCA takedown notices received
  • Content removed due to copyright infringement
  • Users who have had content removed
  • Repeat infringement patterns

5.2 Account Termination

Accounts may be terminated after:

  • Three valid DMCA notices within a 12-month period
  • Failure to respond to multiple infringement notices
  • Pattern of willful copyright infringement

6. Service Provider Status

Safe Harbor Protection

As a service provider under the DMCA, FreeStream qualifies for safe harbor protection when we promptly remove infringing content upon proper notification.

6.1 DMCA Safe Harbor Requirements

To maintain safe harbor protection, we:

  • Designate a DMCA agent with the Copyright Office
  • Implement a repeat infringer policy
  • Respond promptly to valid DMCA notices
  • Do not have actual knowledge of infringing activity
  • Do not receive financial benefit from infringing activity

7. False Claims and Penalties

Warning: False Claims

Knowingly misrepresenting that material is infringing may result in liability for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees.

7.1 Penalties for False Claims

Under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents:

  • That material is infringing, or
  • That material was removed by mistake

May be liable for damages including costs and attorneys' fees.

7.2 Legal Consequences

We reserve the right to pursue legal action against anyone who submits false DMCA claims.
